Desktop colour scheme

The palette shared by the waybar, the rofi launcher, GTK apps (pavucontrol), Qt apps (Dolphin), Konsole and VS Code. Every value below was read out of the live config file it is attributed to, not transcribed by hand.

This page is itself drawn in the scheme. Each label is printed in the colour it describes; because some of those are nearly black and some nearly white, every label sits on whichever of two neutral panels gives it contrast, and the ratio achieved is shown beside it. That is why the small panels are there rather than the labels sitting straight on the page.
